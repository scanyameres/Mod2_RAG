# Velmar Parking Ordinance (VPO)

State of Velmar · in force since 1 January 2025

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## Art. 1 Purpose

This ordinance sets the number and the layout of the parking spaces required by Art. 12 of the Velmar Building Code (VBC).

## Art. 2 Required number of car parking spaces

(1) The following numbers apply:

| Use | Required car parking spaces |
|---|---|
| Detached house | 1 per dwelling; 2 per dwelling if the dwelling has more than 140 m² of living area |
| Apartment building | 1 per dwelling, plus 1 visitor space for every full 10 dwellings |
| Office building | 1 per 40 m² of usable floor area |
| Retail premises with more than 100 m² of sales area | 1 per 30 m² of sales area |
| Retail premises with up to 100 m² of sales area | 2 in total |

(2) Fractions are rounded up to the next whole number.

(3) Municipal zone statutes may set other numbers (Art. 12 (5) VBC).

## Art. 3 Required number of bicycle parking spaces

| Use | Required bicycle parking spaces |
|---|---|
| Detached house | none |
| Apartment building | 2 per dwelling |
| Office building | 1 per 80 m² of usable floor area |
| Retail premises | 1 per 100 m² of sales area |

## Art. 4 Dimensions

(1) The standard car parking space is 2.50 m wide and 5.00 m long (Art. 12 (2) VBC).

(2) A car parking space with a wall or column along one long side must be at least 2.65 m wide. With walls along both long sides it must be at least 2.90 m wide.

(3) A bicycle parking space must be at least 0.70 m wide and 2.00 m long.

## Art. 5 Greenery

On open parking areas, one tree must be planted for every 5 car parking spaces.

## Art. 6 Mixed-use buildings

(1) In mixed-use buildings the required numbers for each use are added.

(2) If the uses need their spaces at different times of day, for example offices and dwellings, the building authority may reduce the total by up to 20 %.

## Art. 7 Transitional provision

(1) This ordinance replaces the Parking Ordinance of 2009.

(2) For building applications submitted before 1 January 2025, the earlier ratio of 1 car parking space per 30 m² of usable floor area continues to apply to office buildings.
