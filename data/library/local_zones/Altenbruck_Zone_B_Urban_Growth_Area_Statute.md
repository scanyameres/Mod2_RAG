# Zone B Statute: Urban Growth Area

Municipality of Altenbruck · adopted 3 September 2024

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## § 1 Area and purpose

Zone B covers the new quarters along the tram line, as shown on the zoning map. The zone is planned for dense, mixed-use development with little car traffic.

## § 2 Permitted uses

(1) Permitted are apartment buildings, office buildings and retail premises.

(2) New detached houses are not permitted.

## § 3 Building form

(1) Buildings must have at least 3 full storeys. The building height must not be more than 22.00 m.

(2) Along main streets, ground floors must have a clear height of at least 4.00 m, so that they can be used for shops.

(3) Flat roofs of more than 100 m² must be green roofs.

(4) Facade materials are free, except for mirrored glass, which is not permitted.

## § 4 Parking

(1) The numbers of car parking spaces required by Art. 2 of the Velmar Parking Ordinance are reduced by 30 %. On plots within 400 m of a tram stop they are reduced by 50 %. The result is rounded up.

(2) Not more than 0.8 car parking spaces per dwelling may be built.

(3) Not more than 10 car parking spaces per plot may be open surface spaces. All further spaces must be in underground or multi-storey garages.

(4) 2 bicycle spaces per dwelling are required, plus 1 space for cargo bikes for every 10 dwellings.

(5) A car-sharing space replaces 4 required car parking spaces. Not more than 20 % of the required spaces may be replaced in this way.

## § 5 Sanitary rooms and water

(1) Bathrooms without windows inside the building are permitted. Their extract fans must be controlled by humidity.

(2) Buildings with more than 20 dwellings need separate pipes for greywater, so that it can be reused later.

(3) Retail premises with more than 800 m² of sales area must keep their customer toilets open to the public during opening hours.

## § 6 Noise and ventilation

Bedrooms facing a main street need mechanical ventilation, so that they can be aired with the windows closed.

## § 7 Safety

Inner courtyards that the fire brigade needs to reach must be built to carry vehicles of 16 tonnes.
