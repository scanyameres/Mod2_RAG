# Zone A Statute: Agricultural Area

Municipality of Altenbruck · adopted 12 May 2021

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## § 1 Area and purpose

Zone A covers the farmland and hamlets outside the built-up town, as shown on the zoning map. The zone is reserved for farming and keeps the rural landscape open.

## § 2 Permitted uses

(1) Permitted are farm buildings, and dwellings for the farm operator, family members and retired farmers, with not more than 2 dwellings per farm.

(2) Farm shops are permitted with a sales area of up to 100 m².

(3) Offices are permitted only as part of a farm.

(4) Apartment buildings, other retail premises and office buildings are not permitted.

## § 3 Building form and materials

(1) The ridge height must not be more than 9.00 m for dwellings and 12.00 m for agricultural halls.

(2) Roofs must be pitched at 30° to 45° and covered with red or brown tiles. Agricultural halls may also use non-reflective metal sheets.

(3) Facades must be timber cladding or render in earth tones. Glossy and reflective materials are not permitted.

## § 4 Parking

(1) 2 car parking spaces are required per dwelling. A farm shop needs 3 spaces in total.

(2) Parking areas must have an unsealed surface such as gravel or grass pavers. Asphalt is permitted only on yards used by heavy machinery.

(3) Underground garages are not permitted. Carports must be built of timber.

(4) Places for agricultural machinery do not count as parking spaces.

## § 5 Sanitary rooms and wastewater

(1) Buildings that cannot be connected to the public sewer need their own small treatment plant or a sealed collection tank.

(2) Accommodation for seasonal workers needs 1 toilet for every 10 workers and 1 shower for every 8 workers.

(3) Farm shops need a toilet for employees and a hand-wash basin. Customer toilets are not required.

## § 6 Distances and emissions

Buildings for livestock must keep a distance of at least 50 m from dwellings that do not belong to the farm.

## § 7 Safety

(1) Slurry pits and ponds must be fenced to a height of 1.80 m.

(2) Farms without a fire hydrant within 300 m need a fire water reserve of at least 48 m³.
