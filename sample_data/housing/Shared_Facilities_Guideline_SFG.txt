Shared Facilities Guideline for Apartment Buildings (SFG)

State of Velmar, Housing Department, edition 2022

Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

1 Purpose and status

This guideline gives recommendations for the shared rooms of apartment buildings and apartment complexes: garages, laundry rooms, bicycle rooms and waste rooms.
It is not binding. A value of this guideline becomes binding only where a regulation or a building permit refers to it.

2 Shared garages

2.1 Dimensions

The clear height of a shared garage should be at least 2.10 m, also below pipes and beams.
Ramps should not be steeper than 15 %. Ramps that are open to the weather should not be steeper than 10 %.
Driving lanes should be 6.00 m wide.

2.2 Ventilation

Garages with a floor area of up to 100 m² can be ventilated naturally through openings in the door.
Larger closed garages should have mechanical ventilation that removes 6 m³/h for every m² of floor area.
Garages with a floor area of more than 1,000 m² should have a carbon monoxide warning system.

2.3 Safety and lighting

Driving lanes and parking spaces should be lit with at least 75 lux.
Walking routes to the exits should be marked on the floor.
Convex mirrors should be placed at bends that cannot be overlooked.

3 Laundry and drying rooms

3.1 Equipment

One washing machine place should be provided for every 6 dwellings.
The room needs a floor drain and a floor covering of slip resistance class R10.

3.2 Ventilation

Laundry and drying rooms without a window should have mechanical ventilation that removes 60 m³/h.

4 Bicycle and stroller rooms

4.1 Size and access

Plan 1.5 m² of floor area for every bicycle space.
The room should be reachable from outside without steps. Doors should be at least 1.00 m wide.

5 Waste rooms

Waste rooms inside the building should have a washable floor, a water tap and a floor drain.
They should be ventilated directly to the outside and should not open onto the stairwell.
