# Housing Standards Regulation (HSR)

State of Velmar · version of 1 July 2023

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## Art. 1 Scope

(1) This regulation applies to dwellings in detached houses and in apartment buildings.

(2) It does not apply to hotels, hostels and other accommodation for guests. These are covered by the Accommodation Premises Regulation.

## Art. 2 Size of dwellings

(1) A dwelling must have at least 28 m² of living area.

(2) Every dwelling needs at least one habitable room of 14 m² or more. No habitable room may be smaller than 9 m².

## Art. 3 Bathrooms and toilets

(1) Every dwelling must have a bathroom with a bathtub or shower, a washbasin and a toilet.

(2) The bathroom must have a floor area of at least 4.0 m². If the toilet is in a separate room, 3.0 m² is sufficient for the bathroom, and the toilet room must have at least 1.2 m².

(3) Dwellings with four or more habitable rooms need a second toilet in a separate room.

## Art. 4 Ventilation of bathrooms and toilet rooms

(1) Bathrooms and toilet rooms need a window with an openable area of at least 0.4 m², or mechanical extract ventilation.

(2) Mechanical extract ventilation must remove at least 40 m³/h from a bathroom and at least 20 m³/h from a toilet room. Fans that switch on with the light must keep running for at least 15 minutes after it is switched off.

(3) Bathrooms without a window are permitted only with mechanical extract ventilation.

(4) The extracted air must be led to the outside. It must not be released into the attic, the stairwell or another room.

## Art. 5 Materials in bathrooms

(1) The whole bathroom floor must be waterproofed. Walls around the bathtub and shower must be waterproofed up to a height of 2.00 m.

(2) Floor coverings must have slip resistance class R10 or better.

(3) Wood-based boards may be used only in a moisture-resistant grade.

## Art. 6 Safety in bathrooms

(1) Electrical sockets must be at least 0.60 m away from the edge of the bathtub or shower and be protected by a residual current device of 30 mA.

(2) Hot water at bathtubs and showers must be limited to 60 °C.

(3) Bathroom and toilet doors must be openable from the outside in an emergency.

## Art. 7 Additional rules for apartment buildings

(1) Every dwelling needs a storage room of at least 5 m² outside the dwelling.

(2) Apartment buildings need a room for bicycles and strollers at ground level.

(3) Apartment buildings with 6 or more dwellings need a playground for small children on the plot.

(4) The number of parking spaces is set by the Velmar Parking Ordinance (VPO). Recommendations for shared garages and other shared rooms are given in the Shared Facilities Guideline (SFG).

## Art. 8 Additional rules for detached houses

(1) A garage or carport may be built directly on the plot boundary if it is not longer than 9.00 m and not higher than 3.00 m at the boundary.

(2) The driveway in front of a garage must be at least 5.00 m long. It may count as the second required parking space of the same dwelling, but not as a parking space of another dwelling.
