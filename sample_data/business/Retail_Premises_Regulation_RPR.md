# Retail Premises Regulation (RPR)

State of Velmar · version of 1 January 2024

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## Art. 1 Scope and size classes

(1) This regulation applies to retail premises (shops and stores).

(2) Retail premises are divided into three classes by their sales area:

| Class | Sales area |
|---|---|
| Small shop | up to 400 m² |
| Medium store | more than 400 m² and up to 2,000 m² |
| Large store | more than 2,000 m² |

## Art. 2 Sales rooms

(1) Sales rooms must have a clear height of at least 3.00 m. In small shops 2.50 m is sufficient.

(2) Main aisles must be at least 1.50 m wide and side aisles at least 1.00 m.

## Art. 3 Customer toilets

(1) Small shops do not need customer toilets.

(2) Medium stores need at least one toilet for women, one toilet for men and one accessible toilet for customers.

(3) Large stores need one more toilet for women and one more for men for every further 1,000 m² of sales area above 2,000 m².

(4) Customer toilets do not count as toilets for employees. Toilets for employees are required by the Workplace Premises Regulation (WPR).

## Art. 4 Ventilation

(1) Sales rooms without openable windows need mechanical ventilation of at least 4 m³/h for every m² of sales area.

(2) Customer toilet rooms are ventilated according to Art. 4 WPR, with at least 30 m³/h per toilet.

## Art. 5 Floors

(1) Floors of sales rooms must have slip resistance class R9.

(2) Areas where fresh food is sold over the counter, such as fish and meat counters, need class R11.

(3) Entrances need a dirt-trapping mat that is at least 1.50 m long in the walking direction.

## Art. 6 Customer parking

(1) The number of parking spaces is set by the Velmar Parking Ordinance (VPO).

(2) Customer parking spaces must be at least 2.70 m wide and 5.00 m long, so that shopping can be loaded with the doors fully open.

(3) Car parks with more than 50 spaces need one shelter for shopping trolleys for every 50 spaces and marked walking routes at least 1.50 m wide.

(4) Medium and large stores need a delivery area that is separate from the customer parking.

## Art. 7 Safety

(1) Sales rooms with more than 100 m² of sales area need at least two exits that lie as far apart as possible.

(2) Glass doors and glass walls must be marked at eye level.

(3) Large stores need a sprinkler system (Art. 10 of the Fire Safety Regulation).
