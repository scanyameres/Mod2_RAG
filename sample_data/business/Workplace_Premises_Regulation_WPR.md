# Workplace Premises Regulation (WPR)

State of Velmar · version of 1 January 2024

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## Art. 1 Scope

(1) This regulation applies to buildings and rooms where employees work, in particular offices.

(2) For retail premises the Retail Premises Regulation (RPR) applies in addition.

## Art. 2 Office rooms

(1) An office room must have at least 8 m² of floor area for the first workstation and 6 m² for every further workstation.

(2) The clear height must be at least:

| Floor area of the room | Clear height |
|---|---|
| up to 50 m² | 2.50 m |
| more than 50 m² | 2.75 m |
| more than 100 m² | 3.00 m |

(3) Office rooms are ventilated through windows, or mechanically with at least 36 m³/h of outdoor air per person.

## Art. 3 Toilet rooms for employees

(1) The number of toilets depends on the number of employees present at the same time:

| Employees | Toilets |
|---|---|
| up to 5 | 1 |
| 6 to 10 | 2 |
| 11 to 25 | 3 |
| 26 to 50 | 5 |
| every further 30 | 1 more |

(2) One washbasin is required for every two toilets, and at least one.

(3) With up to 9 employees, the toilets may be in one room used by all genders. With 10 or more employees, separate toilet rooms for women and men are required.

(4) The walking distance from any workstation to the nearest toilet must not be more than 50 m.

## Art. 4 Ventilation of sanitary rooms

(1) Toilet rooms need mechanical extract ventilation of at least 11 m³/h for every m² of floor area.

(2) Washrooms and shower rooms need at least 15 m³/h for every m² of floor area.

(3) A window alone is sufficient only for a toilet room with a single toilet.

## Art. 5 Floors and walls

(1) Floors must have slip resistance class R9 in offices and toilet rooms, and R10 in washrooms and shower rooms.

(2) Walls of toilet rooms must be washable up to a height of 2.00 m.

## Art. 6 Safety

(1) Escape routes must have emergency lighting.

(2) Doors of toilet cubicles must open outwards, or it must be possible to lift them out from the outside.

(3) A first-aid kit must be available on every floor.

## Art. 7 Parking and cyclists

(1) The number of parking spaces is set by the Velmar Parking Ordinance (VPO).

(2) Workplaces with more than 50 employees need one shower for cyclists for every 50 employees.
