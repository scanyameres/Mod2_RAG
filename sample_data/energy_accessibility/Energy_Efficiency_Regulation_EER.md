# Energy Efficiency Regulation (EER)

State of Velmar · version of 1 January 2025

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## Art. 1 Scope

(1) This regulation applies to new heated buildings and to major renovations of heated buildings.

(2) It does not apply to agricultural operating buildings such as barns, stables and machinery halls.

(3) For protected historic buildings it applies only as far as the measures do not damage the historic substance or appearance.

## Art. 2 Thermal insulation

The heat transfer coefficient (U-value) of building parts must not be higher than:

| Building part | Maximum U-value |
|---|---|
| External walls | 0.24 W/(m²K) |
| Roof | 0.20 W/(m²K) |
| Windows | 1.3 W/(m²K) |
| Ground floor slab | 0.30 W/(m²K) |

## Art. 3 Ventilation systems

(1) Ventilation systems that supply and extract air for a whole dwelling must recover at least 75 % of the heat of the extracted air.

(2) Extract fans that serve only a bathroom or toilet room are exempt from heat recovery if they are controlled by humidity or presence.

(3) Ventilation systems of non-residential buildings with an air flow of more than 4,000 m³/h must recover at least 70 % of the heat.

## Art. 4 Parking and electric vehicles

(1) Residential buildings with more than 5 car parking spaces must have cable ducts to every space.

(2) Non-residential buildings with more than 6 car parking spaces must have cable ducts to every third space and at least one charging point.

(3) Open parking areas with more than 35 spaces must have solar panels above at least half of the spaces.

(4) Garages must have LED lighting with presence control.

## Art. 5 Solar duty for roofs

(1) New buildings must have solar panels on at least 30 % of the roof area.

(2) The duty does not apply where a zone statute prohibits solar installations on the roof surfaces concerned.
