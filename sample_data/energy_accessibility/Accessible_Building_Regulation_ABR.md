# Accessible Building Regulation (ABR)

State of Velmar · version of 1 October 2023

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## Art. 1 Scope

(1) The following must be accessible without barriers:

- buildings open to the public, such as retail premises and offices with visitor traffic;
- workplaces with more than 20 employees;
- apartment buildings with more than 4 dwellings: the dwellings of at least one storey must be barrier-free.

(2) Detached houses are exempt.

## Art. 2 Accessible parking spaces

(1) An accessible car parking space must be at least 3.50 m wide and 5.00 m long.

(2) Buildings covered by Art. 1 need at least 1 accessible parking space, and 1 more for every 25 required car parking spaces.

(3) Accessible parking spaces must be not more than 50 m from the accessible entrance, and the route to it must have no steps.

## Art. 3 Accessible sanitary rooms

(1) An accessible sanitary room needs a free turning area of 1.50 m by 1.50 m.

(2) The door must have a clear width of at least 0.90 m and must open outwards.

(3) The toilet seat must be 46 cm to 48 cm high, with folding grab rails on both sides and a free space of at least 0.90 m beside the toilet.

(4) Showers must be level with the floor and at least 1.20 m by 1.20 m.

(5) Accessible toilets in buildings open to the public need an emergency call cord that can be reached from the floor.

## Art. 4 Where accessible sanitary rooms are required

(1) Buildings open to the public need at least one accessible toilet. It may be used by all genders.

(2) Workplaces with more than 20 employees need at least one accessible toilet.

(3) In barrier-free dwellings the bathroom must meet Art. 3 (1), (2) and (4).

## Art. 5 Routes, ramps and lifts

(1) Ramps must not be steeper than 6 % and must be at least 1.20 m wide.

(2) Buildings with more than 3 storeys that are covered by Art. 1 need a lift.

## Art. 6 Exceptions

(1) In existing buildings the building authority may permit deviations if the requirement can only be met at unreasonable cost.

(2) In protected historic buildings, deviations may be permitted where the requirement would damage the historic substance. An accessible toilet remains required in buildings open to the public.
