# Garage Fire Safety Guideline (GFG)

State of Velmar, Fire Protection Office · edition 2023 · binding by reference in Art. 12 of the Fire Safety Regulation (FSR)

> Sample document invented for software testing. Not a real regulation.

## 1 Garage classes

Garages are classed by their floor area, including driving lanes:

| Class | Floor area |
|---|---|
| Small garage | up to 100 m² |
| Medium garage | more than 100 m² and up to 1,000 m² |
| Large garage | more than 1,000 m² |

An open garage has openings to the outside that cannot be closed and make up at least one third of its wall area. Carports count as open garages. All other garages are closed garages.

## 2 Walls, ceilings and doors

Detached small garages have no fire resistance requirement.

A small garage attached to a house needs a wall of 30 minutes fire resistance towards the house, and a self-closing door if there is a direct door into the house.

Medium and large garages need walls and ceilings of 90 minutes fire resistance, made of materials of fire class A.

## 3 Smoke extraction

Open garages need no smoke extraction.

Closed medium garages need smoke vents to the outside of at least 1,000 cm² per parking space.

Closed large garages need mechanical smoke extraction.

## 4 Escape routes

From every parking space an exit or a stairwell must be reachable within 30 m.

Medium and large garages need at least two exits. The entrance ramp counts as an exit only in medium garages.

## 5 Charging points for electric vehicles

Charging points in closed garages need an emergency switch at the garage entrance that cuts off all charging points.

Charging points must not be installed on escape routes.

## 6 Storage

In small garages up to 20 litres of fuel may be stored in closed containers. In medium and large garages no fuel may be stored outside vehicle tanks.

Parking spaces must not be used for storing furniture, paper or other combustible goods.
